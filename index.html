About me

I am a PhD student in mathematics at Michigan State University, advised by Teena Gerhardt. My research interests lie broadly in algebraic topology, algebraic K-theory, and homotopy theory.

Before coming to Michigan State, I completed my M.Sc. and B.S. in mathematics at Koç University. My master's advisor was Burak Özbağcı. Alongside my research, I enjoy learning new mathematics and am deeply interested in teaching and mentoring.

AI Disclosure

AI Disclosure

My views on the use of AI in mathematics, as of October 2026.

I wish to record my personal views on the use of AI in mathematics. I view artificial intelligence, particularly large language models (LLMs) as a valuable research assistant for learning mathematics and exploring ideas. My primary goal in using AI is to deepen my understanding of mathematics, rather than to replace the process of mathematical reasoning.

I may use AI tools to clarify unfamiliar concepts, organize and develop ideas, examine arguments, and generate examples or possible approaches to mathematical problems. However, any mathematical ideas or arguments developed through these interactions must be independently understood, critically assessed, and verified before being incorporated into my work. I do not accept mathematical claims on the authority of AI alone.

The final mathematical content, including its correctness, originality, citations, and exposition, remains entirely my responsibility as a mathematician.

I believe that AI can meaningfully support mathematical discovery while preserving the essential roles of human understanding, intellectual honesty, and mathematical responsibility.
