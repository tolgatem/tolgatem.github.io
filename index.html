About me

I am a PhD student in mathematics at Michigan State University, advised by Teena Gerhardt. My research interests lie broadly in algebraic topology, algebraic K-theory, and homotopy theory.

Before coming to Michigan State, I completed my M.Sc. and B.S. in mathematics at Koç University. My master's advisor was Burak Özbağcı. I am also interested in mathematical exposition, teaching, and mentoring.
